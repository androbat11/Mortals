# Mission

## Purpose
Mortals is a Mortal Kombat fatalities hub. Users watch a 3D animation of a fatality and learn how to perform it through a short tutorial.

## Audience
Casual fans: people who want to watch fatalities and quickly learn the inputs. The experience stays simple, fun, and low friction.

## What the user sees for each fatality
- A 3D Three.js animation of the fatality
- The game it comes from
- What the fatality does
- A tutorial on how to perform it

## Initial scope
A small MVP set of iconic fatalities from one or two games, enough to prove the concept before adding more content.

## Principles
- Watching and learning come first; no accounts, no clutter.
- Responsive by default: every screen works on phones, tablets, and desktops.
- Original stylized low-poly visuals; no copyrighted game assets.
- Content is easy to add: a new fatality is data plus an animation.
